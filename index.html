Quest Format

Immune System: The Decision Cycle

Same full topic, same underlying facts, three completely different jobs to do with them. Each instance below covers the entire immune system — pathogens, barriers, phagocytes, antibodies, vaccines, antibiotics — but frames it through a different real-world role, with a different physical decision activity and a different way of presenting what happened.

🚨 Outbreak Response Commander

Shalom

Allocate 12 resource tokens across six settlements with different real transmissibility profiles, then roll the dice on the ones you couldn't fully protect.

 💰 Vaccine Program Director

Michael

Run a 100-unit national vaccine budget across five competing disease programs using real herd-immunity math — and survive a few budget surprises along the way.

 🩺 Field Triage Medic

Karis

Work a ten-patient shift with only three antibiotic doses, deciding on each patient as their card is drawn — no advance planning allowed.
Why three variations

Shalom, Michael, and Karis share this week's topic, so each gets a genuinely different experience rather than the same content with a different name on it — same process (how pathogens spread, and the layers of defense that fight them), different lens, different build, different final presentation. No two share both a lens and an output format.

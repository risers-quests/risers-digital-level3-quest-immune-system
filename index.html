Meet your quest partners

Mac and Ana are both white blood cells, but they defend your body in completely different ways. Together, nothing gets past them for long.

Mac the Macrophage

A phagocyte — big, fast, and not picky. Mac finds germs and simply swallows them whole. No germ gets a warning.

Ana the Lymphocyte

A precision fighter. Ana studies exactly what a germ looks like, then builds custom weapons — antibodies — made just for it.
